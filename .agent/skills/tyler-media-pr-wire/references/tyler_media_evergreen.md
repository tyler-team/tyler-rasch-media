## 3. Core Flagship Channels, Formats & Initiatives

### A. One Big World Show (원빅월드쇼 / 1BWS)
*Covers both high-impact visual broadcasts and long-form conversational audio/video editions:*
1. **Talking Head Video Series:**
   - 15–20 minute weekly deep-dive geopolitical, macroeconomic, technology security, and global supply chain intelligence broadcast.
   - High visual density, 3-tier incident stacking, multi-perspective balance (Perspective A/B/C), sharp analytical teleprompter delivery, and 4K motion graphic evidence.
2. **Podcast Edition:**
   - Long-form conversational audio and video podcast series.
   - Extended discussions, expert interviews, geopolitical debates, and unfiltered structural analysis exploring the macro forces shaping the modern world.
- **Editorial Philosophy:** Geopolitical realism, structural incentives, game theory, hard power dynamics, industrial strategy, and the "Affectionate Objective Observer."
- **Host:** Tyler Rasch
- **Official URL:** `https://www.youtube.com/@onebigworldshow`

### B. Really Tyler (리얼리 타일러)
- **Format:** Lifestyle, culture, linguistic nuances, and experimental behind-the-scenes vlogs featuring Tyler Rasch.
- **Tone:** Authentic, witty, intellectual curiosity, and relatable everyday exploration.
- **Host:** Tyler Rasch
- **Official URL:** `https://www.youtube.com/@reallytyler`

### C. SME & Startup Co-Prosperity Program (중소기업·스타트업 상생 프로그램)
- **Overview:** Tyler Media's flagship B2B social-impact initiative lowering the marketing barrier for independent Korean SMEs and startups through zero-cost PPL selections on *1BWS*, up to 50% partnership fee support for applicants, and quarterly ecosystem networking events (in collaboration with partners like D.CAMP).
- **Official URL:** `https://www.tylerrasch.com/sme-startup-program`
